NYFURION ROADMAP

The complete journey of Nyfurion, from concept to reality — every milestone that shaped the project.

Version 1.0

Initial Collection Launch

Release: 2025 ✓ Completed

The Nyfurion Series began with the launch of the first 100 NFT fragments. Each fragment represents a unique element of the narrative universe, giving life to a collection that blends digital art and storytelling. This first wave marked the beginning of Nyfurion, attracting interaction on social media.

Goal achieved: Project foundation and first contact with the public.

Version 1.1

Gradual Expansion of the Collection

Period: 2025 ✓ Completed

Following the initial launch, the Nyfurion collection adopted a structured and progressive release strategy. NFTs were minted in batches of 100 pieces at a time, eventually reaching a total of 400 unique fragments. This expansion allowed the project to maintain high quality and consistency, reinforcing Nyfurion's creative vision.

At the same time, the presence on Instagram began to grow, with each release accompanied by visual content, teasers, and updates. Social media started to reflect the evolution of the Nyfurion universe, creating an initial point of contact with the public.

Goal achieved: Controlled expansion of the collection and launch of visual communication on social platforms.

Version 1.2

Narrative and Visual Expansion

Period: 2025 ✓ Completed

The Nyfurion collection took another evolutionary step with the introduction of new female characters, iconic locations, and narrative elements that had been previously announced. Visual fragments of the destroyed city, forgotten streets, and immersive settings were added to deepen the atmosphere of the Nyfurion universe.

This phase fulfilled earlier promises made to the community, reinforcing the project's narrative and visual consistency. Each new NFT is a piece that enriches the mythology of Nyfurion.

Goal achieved: Thematic and narrative expansion of the collection, with the integration of previously announced content.

Version 1.3

Hidden Evolutions & Dynamic Lore

⚡ Ongoing

Some minted NFTs hide undisclosed narrative evolutions. A few of these evolutions have already been revealed, while others will unfold gradually through Instagram stories, adding depth and mystery to the Nyfurion universe.

A seemingly good character might conceal a dark soul. A minor fragment could turn out to be central to the story. These evolutions aren't always visible in the attributes, but they're part of a living, dynamic lore that enhances the symbolic and collectible value of each NFT.

Goal: Introduce an unpredictable narrative layer that evolves over time and actively engages the community.

Version 1.4

NYFV2: Fragmented Collection

● Live Mint

NYFV2 is a fragmented collection of 100 unique NFTs, directly connected to the narrative universe of Nyfurion. This series was designed for a live mint on OpenSea, offering collectors the opportunity to enter the world of Nyfurion through selected and mysterious fragments.

Connection to the Main Collection

NYFV2 is not a second volume, but a narrative branch that also includes evolutionary fragments linked to the main collection. Once all 100 pieces are minted, the creative and narrative process will continue within Nyfurion, where new fragments, further evolutions, revelations, and connections between NFTs will unfold.

Goal: Expand access to the Nyfurion universe through a fragmented collection, keeping the lore alive and setting the stage for future evolutions.

Version 1.5

Visual Storytelling & Community

✓ Completed

During this phase, a digital book was published on @Nyfurion_club Instagram, offering an immersive and accessible introduction to the Nyfurion project. Through evocative visuals and carefully crafted texts, the audience can explore the narrative universe and understand the vision behind each NFT.

Goals

Make the project readable and engaging, even for those unfamiliar with crypto or NFTs.

Strengthen the bond with the community through clear and inspiring content.

Open the lore to active participation from holders, while maintaining narrative coherence.

Participatory Lore

Nyfurion fragment holders can contribute to the story with small inputs—suggesting plot twists, narrative branches, or unique details that enrich the world. Each fragment becomes a voice in Nyfurion's fate, without disrupting the core storyline.

Ongoing Activities

Publishing Instagram stories and posts for updates and sneak peeks.

Launching contests to win NFTs, with one already completed and others in development.

Version 1.6

License Publication

✓ Completed

The official Nyfurion license has been published as an Instagram Story, making it easily accessible to anyone interested in understanding the rights and usage rules of the NFT fragments. The license outlines the creative possibilities and limitations, protecting both the project and its holders.

Goals

Provide legal clarity and transparency regarding digital ownership.

Strengthen trust in the project's structure and professionalism.

Make the license accessible in a simple and direct format.

Access: Available via Instagram at @Nyfurion_club, with the license story linked directly for viewing.

Version 1.7

Nyfurion Beyond: Towards TV Series

⚡ Long-term

Nyfurion is not just an NFT collection—it's a narrative universe designed to expand beyond the digital realm. The main goal is to bring the Nyfurion series to television or streaming platforms, collaborating with existing brands and entertainment networks.

Each NYF fragment represents a potential spark of storytelling. If one of these NFTs is selected to appear in the series, its holder will receive a percentage of earnings, to be defined based on future agreements.

Goals

Make Nyfurion known to the point of generating a series on demand.

Reward fragment holders with creative and financial recognition.

Transform the collection into a shared narrative ecosystem.

Important Note: This goal is ambitious and will depend on time, the project's growth, and public interest. But every step taken so far is a building block toward that possibility.

Version 1.8

Collaborations & Commercial Initiatives

⚡ Active

Nyfurion is open to collaborations with artists, creatives, brands, and entrepreneurial ventures that wish to contribute to the expansion of its narrative universe. NFT fragment holders are allowed to launch commercial activities inspired by the project, in accordance with the terms outlined in the official license.

Goals

Encourage the creation of artistic and business initiatives linked to Nyfurion.

Give tangible value to NFT fragments beyond the digital space.

Expand the project's visibility through strategic partnerships.

Holder Rights

As stated in the license published on Instagram, holders may use their fragments for creative projects, merchandising, storytelling, or commercial initiatives, provided they respect the brand's guidelines.

Collaborations: Partnership proposals, artistic projects, or commercial initiatives can be submitted via Instagram DM or email. Each proposal will be evaluated based on its alignment with Nyfurion's vision.
