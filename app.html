Web app

Nothing to download

The same app, installed straight from the browser. Once it is on your Home screen or desktop it behaves like any other app: its own window, full screen, works offline and updates on its own.

Press your device: the installation starts from there
Open this page in Safari or Chrome and press the button above. The app opens full screen.
Tap the Share icon — a square with an arrow pointing up. In Safari it sits in the bar at the bottom; in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu first, then Share.
Scroll the list down to Add to Home Screen. It sits below the sharing options, and this is the step everyone misses. Then tap Add, top right.
The NYF icon appears among your apps. Open it from there and sign in with Google.
Press the button above, in Chrome or Edge.
The browser asks to confirm: accept.
The Nyfurion icon lands on your desktop and the app opens in its own window.
On Android take the app above: it is the full version, with notifications.
You prefer no download? Press the button above in Chrome.
The icon lands on your Home screen, like any other app.
On iPhone the app must be added to the Home screen from the Share menu: Apple asks for that step, in every browser. Notifications work from there, with iOS 16.4 or later.

The app asks for no payment and contains no advertising. Sign-in is with Google and creates a free profile; email updates are a separate, optional choice. Privacy notice
