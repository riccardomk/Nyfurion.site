NYF Duel

The Nyfurion card game: 160 cards, four houses, clashes against real players. It installs straight from the browser on iPhone, Android and computer, and it is exactly the same game as the app.

Once it is on your Home screen it opens full screen with its own icon, like any other app. Same cards, same rules, same account: sign in with Google and your collection follows you.

Install NYF Duel Play now in the browser
Free · sign-in with Google
Open this page in Safari and press Install NYF Duel.
Tap Share — the square with the arrow pointing up, in the bar at the bottom.
Scroll the list down to Add to Home Screen, then tap Add, top right.
Open NYF Duel from its icon and sign in with Google.
Open this page in Chrome and press Install NYF Duel.
Press Install in the panel that opens (or ⋮ → Install app).
The NYF Duel icon lands on your Home screen: open it and sign in with Google.
Press Install NYF Duel in Chrome or Edge.
Confirm Install: the game opens in its own window and its icon lands on your desktop.
On iPhone the game must be added to the Home screen from the Share menu: Apple asks for that step, in every browser.
