01

How it works.

Two players, one board, one number that decides every clash. The rules take five minutes; the depth lasts the whole match.

40cards

The deck

28 Entities and 12 Relics. At most 2 copies of the same card and at most 3 pairs. Four houses to choose from.

4+3

The board

Up to 4 Entities and 3 Relics on the field. Attacks open once both players have brought 2 Entities.

MT

The clash

Every Entity carries its MT. The higher MT wins and the difference hits the opponent’s Force Field. Relics can turn it around.

0

Victory

Both players start with 40 Force Field. Take the other to zero and the match is yours.
