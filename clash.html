Nyfurion Clash

The Nyfurion card game: 160 cards, four houses, clashes against real players. It installs straight from the browser on iPhone, Android and computer: this is the only version, always up to date and the same for every player.

Nothing to download from a store

Once it is on your Home screen it opens full screen with its own icon, like any other app. Same cards, same rules, same account: sign in with Google and your collection follows you.

Install Nyfurion Clash Play now in the browser
Free · sign-in with Google
Open this page in Safari and press Install Nyfurion Clash.
Tap Share — the square with the arrow pointing up, in the bar at the bottom.
Scroll the list down to Add to Home Screen, then tap Add, top right.
Open Nyfurion Clash from its icon and sign in with Google.
Open this page in Chrome and press Install Nyfurion Clash.
Press Install in the panel that opens (or ⋮ → Install app).
The Nyfurion Clash icon lands on your Home screen: open it and sign in with Google.
Press Install Nyfurion Clash in Chrome or Edge.
Confirm Install: the game opens in its own window and its icon lands on your desktop.
On iPhone the game must be added to the Home screen from the Share menu: Apple asks for that step, in every browser.
Playing with the app from the Play Store? The game now lives here. Install it from the browser and sign in with the same Google account: your profile, credits and cards are all there. Then you can uninstall the old app.
