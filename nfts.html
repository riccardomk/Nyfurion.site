Questions · Answers

Before you claim.

Six straight answers about what a fragment is, and what it is not.

Read the full license →

Commercial license · Ethereum · Holder Archive

01Is a fragment an investment?

No. A fragment is collectible digital art with usage rights. It is not a financial product, and nothing on this site is financial advice.

02What do I own?

The NFT and a commercial license on the artwork of your fragment. The Nyfurion universe (world, lore, names, brand) stays with its creator. Read the license

03What do I receive?

The image, the character sheet, the 3D card ready to print and the commercial license. Some characters also include a photo pack. See the Journey

04How do I open the vault?

Go to the Holder section, connect the wallet that holds your fragment and sign a free message. The first time only, you register on-chain, with a small network fee.

05What happens if I sell it?

The license moves with the NFT to the new owner. Your rights end at the moment of the transfer.

06And if Nyfurion becomes a series?

Only if an official series is produced, the license sets aside 10% of the creator's net revenues for verified holders of the characters with a main role. It is not a promise of profit. License, Section 6
