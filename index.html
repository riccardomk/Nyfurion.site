The Saga

A Kingdom Born to Last Forever

Once, the realm of Nyfurion knew only peace, under the guidance of a wise king: Ash. But when a slow illness sentenced him to death, he was forced to choose a single heir among his three children, a decision that tormented him, full of doubt, to his final breath. That choice shattered the family and lit a war that still burns. One son, betrayed, became darkness. The queen, Ash's wife, vanished without a trace. And at the heart of the realm, the Unique Portal guards a power capable of rewriting reality itself.
