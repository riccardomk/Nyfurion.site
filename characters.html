The First True King

Ash

Founder of the Realm · Father of the Three

Born an orphan of an ancient bloodline, Ash rose to become the first true King of Nyfurion, choosing to protect rather than to rule. When a slow illness sentenced him to death, he was forced to name a single heir among his three children. The choice would shatter his family and ignite the war that still burns.

"Power without love is only destruction. I choose to protect, not to rule."

Evolution I The beginning. Left at the castle gates as a child, Ash grew up among its halls as a guest more than a son. He made the great library his refuge, teaching himself to read through tales of kings and ancient wars, and slipping out to the villages to learn the hearts of his people. A quiet, curious boy, already carrying the blood of a king.

The Legendary Supreme

Tyri

Leader of Order

The chosen heir. Pure of heart, Tyri never sought the throne, yet his father named him successor over his elder brother, to protect the kingdom from the dark. When grief and war fell upon Nyfurion, he summoned the Guardians of the Portal and raised the Young Warriors of Peace, the last shield between the realm and the shadow of his own brother.

"It is not destiny that guides us. It is our choices."

Evolution I The second son of Ash, still a child. A pure heart raised on equality and respect, he dreamed of one thing only: a realm that would live in peace forever.
