The Chronicle of the Realm

The Lore of
Nyfurion

A realm suspended between light and darkness. A kingdom born to last forever, until a single choice set it ablaze.

▾
Act One

The War of Balance

❖

Nyfurion, the realm suspended between light and darkness, was not always a battlefield. Once, under the guidance of a wise king, Ash, it thrived in harmony. No wars. No divisions. A kingdom born to last forever.

Ash · The First True King
"Power without love is only destruction. I choose to protect, not to rule."

But peace is never eternal. A slow illness crept into the King, sentencing him to death. Tormented and full of doubt, Ash was forced to choose a single heir among his three children.

He chose the one who carried discipline, justice, and inner strength: Tyri, the Legendary Supreme. That choice would shatter a family, and light a war that still burns.

Act Two

The Rise of Dark Power

❖

The firstborn, the most ambitious, the most tormented, had awaited that throne his entire life. When he was passed over, rage consumed him. A void opened within. A thirst for revenge. That day, Nyv, the Dark Power of Evil, was born.

Nyv · The Dark Power of Evil
"I am what remains when hope dies."

He tore open time itself and built an empire of shadow beyond the rift. He gathered those who refused to be ruled: the Baby Rebels, the DPOE, young warriors who believe chaos is freedom.

And yet, one figure watches from the shadows. The Queen, Rey. The daughter who holds the last spark of primordial power. She takes no side. But if Nyfurion falls, she alone will decide its fate.

Act Three

The Guardians of the Portal

❖

As conflict spread, Tyri understood he could not stand alone. Armies were not enough. Courage was not enough. The heart of Nyfurion had to be sealed, for at the center of the realm lay a forbidden gateway: the Unique Portal.

No one knew what waited beyond it. Only that it held a power capable of rewriting reality itself. Should Dark Power reach it, the realm would fall. And so the Guardians of the Portal were chosen, sworn to defend the gateway with their very existence.

Act Four

The Shadow Predators

❖
The Shadow Predators
"They serve neither light nor dark. Only the fall."

As war intensifies, a quieter danger spreads through the streets. The Shadow Predators, infiltrators with no faction. They do not wish to rule Nyfurion. They only seek to profit from its downfall, stealing all that is precious from the homes of neutrals, weakening them, isolating them.

No one sees them. No one stops them.

Act Five

The Fate of Nyfurion

❖

Now the realm teeters on the brink of ruin. Tyri fights to protect order. Nyv advances, dragging the Rebels with him. The Guardians hold the seal. The Young Warriors of Peace struggle to preserve all that remains.

Rey · The Queen who must choose
"Enough."

But all of this may not be enough. Because the Queen has yet to choose. If she intervenes, Nyfurion may find a new balance. If she remains in the shadows, the conflict will be eternal.

And nothing in Nyfurion is truly safe anymore.
