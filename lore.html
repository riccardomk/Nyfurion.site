Nyfurion Lore

Chapter I

The War of Balance and the Fate of the Guardians

Nyfurion, the realm suspended between light and darkness, was not always a battlefield. Once, under the guidance of the King of Nyfurion, it thrived in harmony—no wars, no divisions. The sovereign, gifted with unparalleled wisdom, knew he could not rule forever. He had to choose an heir.

He was the father of three children, each with a different vision of the world. When the day of succession arrived, he chose the eldest—the one who demonstrated discipline, justice, and inner strength: the Legendary Supreme. But this decision did not come without consequences.

Chapter II

The Rise of Dark Power

The second born, the most ambitious, the most tormented, had long awaited the day he would inherit power. When he was excluded, he was overcome with rage. A void grew within him, a deep thirst for revenge. That day, the Dark Power of Evil was born—the one who would defy order and shatter balance.

He refused to accept his father's decision. He gathered followers among those who did not want to be ruled, those who saw the Legendary Supreme as a tyrant disguised as a savior. Thus, the Baby Rebels emerged—young warriors who despise imposed order and reject civilization. To them, chaos is freedom.

But in this war between good and evil, there is one figure watching from the shadows: The Queen, the third descendant, who has never fought but holds in her hands the last spark of primordial power. She takes no side, but if Nyfurion falls, she will decide the kingdom's fate.

Chapter III

The Creation of the Guardians

As conflict spread, the Legendary Supreme realized he could not face it alone. Armies were not enough. Courage was not enough. The heart of Nyfurion had to be sealed.

At the center of the realm existed a forbidden gateway: the Unique Portal. No one knew what lay beyond it, but it was said to contain absolute power—something capable of rewriting reality itself. If Dark Power reached it, Nyfurion would fall.

That was when The Guardians of the Portal were chosen. They were not ordinary warriors. Each had followed a precise path, honed their skills, and sacrificed their existence to protect the gateway. The Eternal Guardian, the only one who has seen the Portal open and close. He survived and still awaits the day when it will be needed again.

Chapter IV

The Shadow Predators and the Silent War

As war intensifies, another danger grows in the streets of Nyfurion. The Shadow Predators are infiltrators with no faction. They serve neither good nor evil but steal everything of value from the homes of neutrals, weakening and isolating them.

They do not wish to rule Nyfurion. They only seek to profit from its downfall. No one sees them. No one stops them.

Chapter V

The Fate of Nyfurion

Now, the realm teeters on the brink of ruin. The Legendary Supreme fights to protect order. Dark Power advances, dragging the Rebels with him. The Guardians maintain the seal on the Portal. The Baby Warriors of Peace struggle to preserve civilization, guided by the Masters of Discipline.

But all this may not be enough. Because the Queen has yet to choose. If she intervenes, Nyfurion may find a new balance. If she remains in the shadows, conflict will be eternal.

But nothing in Nyfurion is truly safe anymore.
